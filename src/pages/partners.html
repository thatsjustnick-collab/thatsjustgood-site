Questions

Good questions

What is the Corporate Good Council?

A yearly membership for companies that includes a private impact event for up to 50 people, a council seat with quarterly roundtables and an annual summit, a year of My Impact Hub, two volunteer outings, a planning session, a year-end impact report and a podcast spotlight. It's $25,000 a year.

Which causes can we support?

Any cause you care about: hunger, education, first responders, children's hospitals, animals, the environment and more. We match you with local nonprofit partners.

Do you work with companies outside Dallas-Fort Worth?

Yes. We're based in Dallas-Fort Worth and run programs in every office you have. Travel beyond 30 miles is billed at cost.

How do you price programs?

By number of employees, office locations and how many events and Days of Service you want each year. Add-ons let you scale up as you grow.
