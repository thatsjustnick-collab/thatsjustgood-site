How it works

Up and running in five minutes

  1. Create your group

    Set your hour or giving goal and share one join link or QR code. Work and school emails join automatically.

  2. Log from your phone

    Hours, photos and a short reflection. The supervisor confirms by email, no account needed.

  3. Your report writes itself

    On the 1st of every month you get a finished impact report and a social graphic, ready to share.

Questions

Good questions

What is My Impact Hub?

My Impact Hub by That's Just Good is a platform for tracking volunteer hours, donations and goals. It's free for individuals, and schools, groups and companies subscribe to run their programs on it.

Do members have to pay?

Never. Members and individuals join free with a link, a QR code or their school or work email. Only group and company plans are paid.

How are hours verified?

The supervisor gets an email to confirm the hours, and group leaders approve them. Every transcript has a check code anyone can use to confirm it's real.

What happens when I graduate or change jobs?

Your record lives on your personal profile, so it comes with you. The school or company keeps its own reports.

Is the President's Volunteer Service Award still available?

AmeriCorps paused new awards on May 27, 2025 and hasn't announced a restart date. The Hub keeps counting your hours by age level so you're ready if it returns.

Is student data safe?

We never sell data or show ads. Personal accounts are for ages 13 and up, and parents can see their student's record.
