Our story

Giving back should be the best part of your week

Nick Marino Jr. founded That's Just Good in 2020 to give companies, groups, and families a fun, energized way to make a difference together. Today it's a media and events brand built on one idea: when people do good together, everybody wins.

What we believe

Action and education drive change

Every That's Just Good event pairs hands-on action with a clear picture of who you're helping and why. People don't just pack a box and leave. They understand the need and stay involved long after the event ends.

We work with a network of charities and advocacy organizations so every activity has an immediate impact. If you have a cause that matters to you, we'll build an event around it, and help you share it with your network and local media.

Our podcast and kids programs carry the same idea further: tell the stories of people doing good, and show the next generation they can do it too.

Our founder

Nick Marino Jr.

Founder & Chief Giving Officer

Nick is a social entrepreneur, philanthropist, and lover of life. People say he is "contagiously infected with enthusiasm and passion," and anyone who has been to one of his events knows why.

Born and raised in Garland, Texas, by parents from New York City, Nick has hosted more than 1,100 events around the world that have impacted over 6 million people in need. He leads Tango Charities as Executive Director and Chief Hunger Officer, the organization behind Feed The City and Feed The Country.

He hosts the That's Just Good podcast and wrote the Nico Makes A Difference children's book series. He was named a Top Texan Under 30 in 2016 and a Dallas Business Journal 40 Under 40 honoree in 2025.
