    The books

    Meet Nico

    Nico Makes A Difference is a children's book series by That's Just Good founder Nick Marino Jr. Each story shows kids that helping others is something they can do right now, at any age.

    Every visit leaves kids with a story to remember and a small act of kindness they did together.
