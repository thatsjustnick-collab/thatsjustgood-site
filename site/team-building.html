How it works

Booked in minutes, handled start to finish

Plan online, see your price instantly, and lock your date. We cover the details, keep the room fun, and make sure the event runs without a hiccup.

  1. Plan it online

    Choose your format, cause, group size, date and location. Your price updates as you go.

  2. Lock your date

    Reserve with a deposit, or request a custom quote. We confirm within 1 business day.

  3. Rally your team

    You get one RSVP link for your team and a live headcount. We send everyone reminders.

  4. See your impact

    After the event, you get an impact report with photos and totals, plus volunteer-hour certificates.
