How it works

We set it up. You make the impact.

Bring your people. We bring the idea, the materials, and a host who keeps the energy up from start to finish. Every activity goes straight to a partner charity, so the impact is immediate.

See how events work

  1. Choose your format

    We host only, or we run the whole event, supplies included.

  2. Pick a cause

    Hunger, first responders, pet rescue, cancer survivors, or one you care about.

  3. Set the date and place

    Your office, a local restaurant, or a venue of your choice.
