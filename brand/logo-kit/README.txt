Every folder has three formats:
  svg   Vector. Best for websites, designers, sign makers and embroidery digitizers.
  pdf   Vector. Send this to printers and merch or embroidery vendors.
  png   Picture with a transparent background (2000 px). Use in slides, documents and social posts.

Color versions (in each logo and icon folder):
  full-color            Navy with a coral smile. For white, cloud, sky or sunshine backgrounds.
  reversed-coral        White with a coral smile. For navy or dark photos.
  reversed-sunshine     White with a sunshine smile. For navy; great for kids events.
  reversed-sky          White with a sky smile. For navy; great for community events.
  white-navy-smile      White with a navy smile. For coral backgrounds.
  one-color-navy        All navy. One-color printing and embroidery.
  one-color-white       All white. One-color printing on dark colors.
  one-color-coral       All coral. Merch on white or navy.
  one-color-sky         All sky. Merch on navy.
  one-color-black       All black. Forms, newspapers, vendors that need black.

Icon tiles: navy, sky, coral, sunshine, cloud and white backgrounds, each as a rounded square and a circle.
